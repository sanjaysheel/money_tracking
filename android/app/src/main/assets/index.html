Install on Your Phone

Aura Finance is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It can be installed directly to your phone's home screen with full offline support and no app store needed!

 iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this URL in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow up ⎋).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (⊞).
  4. Tap Add. The Aura Finance icon appears on your home screen!
▶ Android (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open this URL in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Three dots (⋮) menu in the upper right.
  3. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm, and the app launches as an independent fullscreen app!
