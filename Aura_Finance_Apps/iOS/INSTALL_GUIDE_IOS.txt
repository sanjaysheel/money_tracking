========================================================================
AURA FINANCE - iOS (IPHONE & IPAD) INSTALLATION GUIDE
========================================================================

Because Apple iOS strictly requires macOS/Xcode to compile native .ipa files,
Aura Finance provides TWO direct ways to run natively on iPhone & iPad:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1 (RECOMMENDED): DIRECT HOME-SCREEN APP (ZERO MAC / ZERO XCODE NEEDED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aura Finance is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with complete Apple Web Clip
specs, safe-area viewport handling, and standalone display mode.

Step 1:
Double-click "Start_iOS_Server.bat" inside this folder on your PC.
It will start a local server and display your Wi-Fi address, for example:
   http://192.168.1.5:8080

Step 2:
Make sure your iPhone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your PC.
Open Safari on your iPhone and type the address (e.g. http://192.168.1.5:8080).

Step 3:
In iPhone Safari:
1. Tap the Share button (square icon with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (+).
3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.

Result:
Aura Finance is now installed directly onto your iPhone Home Screen with its luxury icon!
When launched from the Home Screen:
- Zero Safari browser address bars or buttons (runs full-screen standalone)
- Full iPhone notch & Dynamic Island safe-area padding
- 100% offline local storage, PIN lock security, and Apple liquid glass physics

(Note: You can also deploy the contents of the "iOS_PWA_Bundle" folder to free hosting like Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages in 30 seconds to access your iPhone app from anywhere in the world!)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: NATIVE XCODE SWIFT PROJECT (FOR MAC USERS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you or your team have a Mac with Xcode:
1. Open the "Xcode_Project_Template" folder.
2. Open "AuraFinance.xcodeproj" in Xcode.
3. Plug in your iPhone via Lightning/USB-C.
4. Select your Team signing certificate and click "Run" (Command + R).
5. Xcode compiles and installs the native app directly to your iPhone as an IPA.
